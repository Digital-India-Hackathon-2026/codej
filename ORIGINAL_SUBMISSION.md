## Idea
CivicPulse is an AI-powered public grievance platform that helps citizens report civic issues quickly and accurately. Instead of asking users to manually choose departments, our AI understands the complaint, classifies it into the correct category, identifies its priority, and routes it to the appropriate government authority.

The platform supports text, voice, and image-based complaints, automatically detects duplicate reports from the same location, and provides real-time tracking for citizens. Government officials receive an intelligent dashboard with complaint hotspots, department-wise analytics, and response timelines, enabling faster decisions and proactive maintenance.

By reducing manual effort, eliminating misclassification, and improving transparency, CivicPulse bridges the communication gap between citizens and government, ensuring faster resolution of public issues and smarter governance.
